## About KeepHer

KeepHer is a relationship management app designed for men to strengthen their relationships through:

- Memory vault for partner preferences and important details
- AI-powered gift and date suggestions
- Relationship coaching through AI conversations
- Important date reminders and notifications
- Relationship health tracking and insights
